Privacy & security

Your data lives with you, not with us

A mileage log shows where you go, when and why. Pritrek keeps it only in places you control — so there’s nothing on our side to lose.

Primary copy

On your phone

Every trip you record is stored locally on your device, so your logbook is always with you.

Private backup

In your Google Drive

Backups go to your own Google Drive — your account, not ours — so a new phone never means a lost log.

Nothing stored

Not with Pritrek

We don’t keep customer data. No Pritrek database of your trips means nothing to leak, sell or hand over.

Record & export

Tax-ready records, ready when you need them

Pritrek records what a tax mileage log typically needs: the date, start and destination, purpose, odometer readings and distance of every trip, and whether it was business, commute or private. Purposes are yours to name and organise.

Exporting a customised logbook — choosing the fields, their order and formatting — is coming soon as a Pro feature. Until then, your full history stays on your phone and backed up to your own Google Drive.

Requirements differ from country to country. Pritrek helps you keep complete records, but it isn’t tax advice — check what applies to you or ask your accountant.
