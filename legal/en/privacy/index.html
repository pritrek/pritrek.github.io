Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-26

This policy explains what Pritrek ("the app", "we") does with your data. The short version: your trips stay on your phone. Pritrek has no server of its own, no advertising and no analytics.

1. Who is responsible and how to reach us

Pritrek is published by an independent developer. The developer's name and address are shown on Pritrek's Google Play page. For any question about your data, write to privacy@pritrek.com.

2. What the app handles

Pritrek records your trips. The following stays on your device:

  • Your location trail: GPS positions with their times. They are recorded continuously while a trip is in progress. Between trips, Pritrek also takes a position about every 5 minutes, in the background and even when the app is closed or not in use, to notice when a drive starts; these are not saved as a trip unless a drive begins. That is how trips are recorded without you keeping the app open. Android shows a notification while Pritrek is running in the background.
  • Stops along a trip, and the place names found for them.
  • Vehicles you add, and the odometer readings you enter.
  • App settings, such as your language, distance unit and recording intervals.

All of this is stored in a database inside the app's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read it.

3. What leaves your device, and why

Only the following leaves your device, each for one purpose:

  • Place names. To name a stop, the app sends that stop's coordinates, one stop at a time and never the whole trail, to your device's map service: Google on Android, Apple on iOS. Their privacy policies apply to that request.
  • Maps. The map is drawn with Google Maps. When a map is shown, Google receives the area being viewed and your device's IP address, as with any use of Google Maps. The Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies.
  • Google sign-in and Google Drive backup. If you sign in with Google and turn on backup, a copy of your data is stored in a private, hidden folder of your own Google Drive that only Pritrek can open. The developer never receives it. Without sign-in nothing is sent to Google Drive.

Pritrek contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting service of its own, and it does not sell your data or share it for advertising. If you allow it in your Google Play settings, Google Play may collect its own usage and crash statistics about the app; that is between you and Google.

4. Why we handle your data, and on what basis

Location is used only to record your trips, draw the route and add up distance: that is the service you asked for. We ask for your consent before the location permission prompts. When you tap "Agree and continue" on the first screen and then allow location in the system dialogs, you consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time by turning off location for Pritrek in your device's settings; the app then stops recording. Withdrawing does not affect what was recorded before. Where the law asks for a legal basis, it is your consent and, for the parts needed to provide the service you asked for, the performance of that service.

5. How long data is kept

Trips, vehicles and odometer readings stay on your device until you delete them, uninstall the app, or clear its storage; each of these removes them for good. A backup stays in your Google Drive until you delete it (in the app, or in the settings of your Google account for hidden app data) or remove the app's access to your Drive.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR (European Economic Area and United Kingdom) and the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights to access, correct, delete and export your data, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because your data is on your own device, you can already exercise these rights yourself: view and edit trips, vehicles and readings in the app, delete them, or uninstall the app. If you would like help, write to privacy@pritrek.com and we will reply within one month. We do not sell personal information.

7. Children

Pritrek is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the European Economic Area), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has used the app, write to privacy@pritrek.com.

8. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read, and every connection the app makes (map tiles, place-name lookup, backup) is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure. If your phone has no screen lock, anyone who can use the phone can open the app.

9. International transfers

Pritrek has no server, so it does not transfer data between countries itself. Google, whose services the app uses as described in section 3, may process data in several countries under its own policies and safeguards.

10. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is handled in a way that needs your consent, the app asks you again. The current version is always at the address you opened. If a translation differs from the English text, the English text prevails.
